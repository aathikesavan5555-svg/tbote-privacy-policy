Privacy Policy

THE BEGINNING OF THE END
Version 1 · November 2026

This policy explains what information THE BEGINNING OF THE END (“the game”) handles, who handles it, and the choices you have. The game is developed and published by ETNIAK Studios (“we”).

In short

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. ETNIAK Studios does not receive, store or sell personal information from the game.
  • Advertising and in-app purchases are provided by Google. When they are used, Google processes some information independently, under its own privacy policy, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a save file in its private storage on your device. It contains your progress, stars, coins and items, your settings and control layout, play statistics such as time played and attempts per level, a local count of game events used to balance the game, and a record of Google Play purchases (the product and the purchase token Google provides) so that each purchase is delivered once.

None of this is sent to ETNIAK Studios. Android’s app-data backup is turned off for this game, so the save is not copied to your Google account backup. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes the save permanently.

Information collected by ETNIAK Studios

None. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos or other personal information, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting service that sends data to us.

Google Play gives developers statistics and crash reports about their apps, such as install counts and crash details with the device model and Android version. Google provides these under its own privacy policy and your device settings. We use them only to understand and fix problems.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob: rewarded videos that you choose to watch, and occasional advertisements between levels.

When advertising is active, Google’s advertising software in the game may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), information about your device and the app, and your interactions with advertisements. Google uses it to show, limit and measure advertisements and to prevent fraud. Google processes this information as an independent provider under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device’s settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Where the law requires your consent for personalised advertising, it is requested separately before such advertising is shown. Accepting the game’s Terms & Conditions, or reading this policy, is not consent to advertising data processing.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms and privacy policy. The game never sees or stores your payment details. Google Play tells the game which product was bought and provides a purchase token, which the game keeps on your device to deliver and keep your items.

Device permissions

The game asks Android for internet and network-state access, used for advertising and purchases, and for vibration, used for the optional haptic feedback. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. ETNIAK Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game does not collect personal information for us.

Keeping and deleting information

Information in the save file stays on your device until you clear the game’s data or uninstall it. ETNIAK Studios holds no personal information from the game, so there is nothing for us to delete. For information processed by Google, use Google’s privacy tools and your device’s advertising settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because ETNIAK Studios does not hold personal information from the game, requests about advertising or purchase data are best made to Google, which processes it. You may still contact us with any privacy question.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The current version is always available in the game under Settings > Privacy & Legal.

Contact

ETNIAK Studios can be contacted through the developer contact details shown on the game’s Google Play store page.
